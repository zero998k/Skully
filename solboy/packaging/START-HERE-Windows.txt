SOLBOY - START HERE (Windows)
=============================

EASIEST: open PowerShell (Start menu > type PowerShell > Enter), paste this line and press Enter:
   irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zero998k/Skully/claude/hopeful-edison-2b8ltm/solboy/scripts/install-windows.ps1 | iex
   It installs SolBoy in Documents\SolBoy, adds a desktop shortcut and opens it. Then skip to step 3.

1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it, like Documents\SolBoy.
   (Right-click the zip > Extract All. Don't run it from inside the zip.)

2. Double-click SolBoy.exe.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   That shows up because SolBoy is a new app without a paid signature.

3. A black window opens and asks a few questions:
   - Your free Helius key: make an account at https://www.helius.dev and copy your API key.
   - The wallets you want to copy: paste one per line, then press Enter on an empty line.
   - SOL per trade: press Enter to use 0.05.
   - (Optional) A free Groq key for the Ask SolBoy assistant: https://console.groq.com/keys

4. Your browser opens the SolBoy dashboard at http://localhost:7777
   SolBoy starts in PAPER mode: real prices, no real money. Let it run a few days.

5. To use real money: open the Account tab, create your SolBoy wallet, send it a small
   amount of SOL, then flip the switch to "Real money". Only use money you can afford to lose.

Keep the black window open while SolBoy runs. Close it to stop SolBoy.
While SolBoy is open it keeps your PC from sleeping by itself (the screen can still turn off).
The full guide is in README.md (open it with Notepad).

Updating later: when a yellow "Update" button appears at the top of the dashboard, click it.
SolBoy updates itself and keeps your settings, keys, wallets and trades.
