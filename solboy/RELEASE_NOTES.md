**New in this version**
- **One-click updates:** when a new SolBoy is out, an Update button appears at the top of the dashboard. Click it and SolBoy updates itself and reloads. Your settings, keys, wallets and trades stay.
- **Quick settings** (Loadout tab): change take profit, stop loss, SOL per buy, daily loss limit and max tokens held instantly. In the terminal, type tp 50 or sl 25.
- **Deeper research on brand-new coins:** the X post the coin links to, Pump.fun data, the creator's past coins and copycat tickers. Research starts the moment a wallet you copy buys.
- Fixed "Transaction version (1) is not supported" on buys.
- Update checks have a backup, so they keep working even when GitHub is busy.

Downloads: **SolBoy-for-Mac.zip** (Mac), **SolBoy-for-Windows.zip** (Windows PC), **SolBoy-for-Server.zip** (24/7 online server).
